---
title: A new look: one color scale, percentile boxes and Summaries upgrades
date: 2026-11-20
type: update
summary: Every chart now uses the same ice-to-fire scale, the cards use percentile boxes, and Summaries gain release points and Stuff+ violins.
tags: [site, mlb, nhl]
---

- **One color scale everywhere.** Ice means below average and orange means above, on every card, table, heatmap and chart, MLB and NHL alike.
- **Percentile boxes.** The MLB cards now use the 100-box strip from the NHL cards, and Pitch+ grades sit in square tiles.
- **Summaries:** two new side-panel options, the pitcher's **release point** for that game or season and a **Stuff+ violin** for each pitch type.
- **This page.** Articles and updates will be posted here.
