As you can see, [pasttheeyetest.com](https://www.pasttheeyetest.com) looks very different. Over the past month or so, I have been deciding on the next steps to take in terms of branding. I had not changed the UI at all since the launch of the JavaScript-integrated version of my site, so I decided to make some changes. Here are all of them.

- This page! This page will first open when you search my website in your browser, or press the link from X. From now on, all model changes, new features, UI/UX changes, and articles on players or model methodology will be right here. My goal is to put out at least two articles a week, one Orioles/Capitals-related, and one all-MLB/all-NHL related.

  <img src="/posts/ui-ux-changes/landing-page.png" alt="The Articles and Updates landing page" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The Articles and Updates Landing Page</figcaption>
</figure>

- The navigation bar got a makeover; instead of the emojis with “MLB” and “NHL” on them at the top, you should now see a full, enlarged sidebar with MLB and NHL logos, with complete dropdowns under each section for their subsections.

<figure>
  <img src="/posts/ui-ux-changes/sidebar.png" alt="The full navigation sidebar" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The full navigation bar</figcaption>
</figure>

- Percentiles/ranks are now colored on what I like to call an “ice to fire” themed scale, with the blue for the lower end being a lighter color, and the higher end being orange. You’ll notice that I also made this uniform for all factions of the site, including the summaries, which were originally scaled uniquely from red → green, and heatmaps.
- Percentiles are now shown in a tick and box format, ridding the classic-style savant bubbles. This was mainly for branding purposes, as I think the savant bubbles are too in-line with the norm and at this point, boring. I think they definitely do convey the data well, but I personally enjoy the burst of color and uniqueness displayed by this tick and box format.
- All player names and subheaders have increased in pixel size to make them more legible.

<figure>
  <img src="/posts/ui-ux-changes/alonso-hitter-card.png" alt="Pete Alonso’s 2026 hitter card" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Pete Alonso’s 2026 hitter card, with new percentile loading</figcaption>

- Pitcher cards got a new look, with the pitch plot being enlarged and there being just one solid dot per pitch type (scaled based on how many times a pitcher threw it), and the pitches at the bottom being highlighted in that pitch’s designated color. Pitch modeling “bubbles” are also squares now, with rounded edges.

<figure>
  <img src="/posts/ui-ux-changes/rasmussen-pitcher-card.png" alt="Drew Rasmussen’s 2026 pitcher card" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Drew Rasmussen’s 2026 pitcher card, with new layout</figcaption>
</figure>

- Pitcher summaries, again on the new “ice to fire” scale, have 2 new sidebar dropdowns. Release point diagrams, from the main pitcher dashboard, have been added as a side panel, and a new feature altogether: the stuff+ violin charts. Unlike the platoon bars, the violins can give a gauge of the stuff+ range a pitcher had within an outing or season. (I also think they just look sick, too)

<figure>
  <img src="/posts/ui-ux-changes/tolle-season-summary.png" alt="Payton Tolle’s 2026 season summary" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Payton Tolle’s 2026 season summary, with release diagram and stuff violin plot</figcaption>
</figure>

- A small change, URLs have also been added to each section of the site, so they are easier to bookmark and come back to, without having to reload the website.

It may look different, but [pasttheeyetest.com](https://www.pasttheeyetest.com) is still the same. The formulas and models are still intact, and the same data as before. If you have any inquiries about anything related to the UI, you can always DM me on X, @pasttheeyetest. Thanks!
